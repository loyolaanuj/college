Privacy Policy

CollegeMerch is a frontend-only demonstration project. This page explains how the demo handles information.

Data Storage

All data (accounts, cart, orders) is stored only in your browser's LocalStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Clearing your browser data removes everything.

No Real Accounts

Login and signup are simulated for demonstration. Passwords are stored in plain text in LocalStorage and should never be considered secure.

No Payments

No real payment information is collected or processed. Checkout is a demonstration only.

Cookies & Tracking

This demo does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Final Legal Wording

The final, official privacy policy is To Be Decided by the college.
