Privacy Policy

NEXUS values the privacy and security of our students, faculty, and alumni. This policy outlines how your information is handled.

Information Collection

When you register or place an order, we collect your name, college or personal email, phone number, and delivery address to fulfill and confirm your orders.

Account Security & Encryption

Passwords are securely hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption before storage. We utilize secure HTTP-only cookies and tokens for session authentication.

Order & Payment Privacy

Payment and delivery records are kept strictly confidential and used solely for fulfilling campus store orders and tracking status.

Cookies & Sessions

We use essential cookies strictly necessary for managing your active session and shopping cart items.
